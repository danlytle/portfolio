The IT Manager's Manifesto

I miss the tactile, 3D design of Windows 98 and the optimistic, glassy skeuomorphism of the Frutiger Aero era. Before everything became "flat" and minimal, tech felt like it had texture, weight, and personality.

This page is a tribute to that era! Startup sounds, dial-up dreams, and the feeling that the future was going to look like glass and water.
